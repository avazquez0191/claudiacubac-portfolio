02About

Hello, I'm Claudia.

I believe the best design is calm, considered and built to last. It should earn attention, not demand it.

For the past eight years I've worked with independent businesses, publishers and cultural institutions on identities and printed matter with clarity and character. Before going independent in 2023, I was Senior Designer at Estudio Plano, leading identity projects for clients across Europe and Latin America.

My process is collaborative and research-driven. I listen first, sketch a lot, and obsess over typography, proportion and the small details that make a brand feel right. Away from the desk I collect vintage type specimens and print at a local risograph studio.
