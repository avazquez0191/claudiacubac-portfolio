02About

Hello, I'm Claudia.

I design brands and stories with a typographer's eye: clear, expressive and made to work everywhere they live, from a business card to a website.

I studied Visual Communication, specialising in Typographic Design, at Havana's Superior Institute of Design (ISDi), where I later taught Typography I and II. My institutional typeface for the school, Cuendias, was a finalist at Tipos Latinos 2015.

Since then I've led brand, campaign, editorial and digital projects for companies, hotels, restaurants and cultural institutions. Today I'm Creative Director at Aisencia GmbH (Bremen University, Germany), working on brand strategy and on UX/UI design for websites and artificial intelligence software.
